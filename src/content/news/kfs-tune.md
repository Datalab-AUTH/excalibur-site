The EXCALIBUR Project is pleased to share the publication of the [journal article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116523) **“KFS-TUNE: Kernel-based Feature Selection for efficiency and accuracy tuning in Time Series Classification”** in Knowledge-Based Systems.<br>
The paper introduces KFS-TUNE, a kernel-based feature selection approach for time series classification that combines convolutional kernels with targeted feature selection. By focusing on the most informative kernel-derived features, the method aims to reduce computational cost while preserving predictive performance.<br><br>
The work contributes to efficient and scalable machine-learning workflows for time series data and aligns with EXCALIBUR’s broader interest in practical, transparent, and human-centric AI pipelines.<br> 

**Authors:** Sofia Vei, Eleftherios Tiakas, Athena Vakali<br> 
**Journal:** *Knowledge-Based Systems* <br>
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116523 <br><br>
